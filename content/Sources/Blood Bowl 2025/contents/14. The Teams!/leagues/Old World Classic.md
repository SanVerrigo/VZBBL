When the NAF collapsed, Blood Bowl struggled in the Old World. That was until the set-up of the Old World Classic, giving teams from all over the Old World a new structured league to play in.
Thanks to huge donations from Imperial benefactors, and constant coverage on Cabalvision, the Old World Classic has become one of the most influential leagues in the known world.
