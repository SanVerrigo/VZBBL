> Many members of the medical profession will ply their trade on a freelance basis, travelling in between the various stadia to offer their services to teams in need of additional patching up - for a fee of course.

A team that has purchased a Wandering Apothecary can use them, once per game, in the same manner as a regular Apothecary.
