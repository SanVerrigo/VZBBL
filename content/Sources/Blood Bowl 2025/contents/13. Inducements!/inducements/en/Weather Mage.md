> The weather is unpredictable at the best of times, and some stadium owners will pay good money to hire a mage that can control the weather - ensuring that fans are kept warm, dry and happy whilst also making sure that the weather is beneficial for their team on the pitch!

Once per game, at the start of any of your Turns, you may immediately make a roll on the Weather Table, applying a modifier of up to +2 or -2 to the roll. The resulting weather conditions are applied immediately and will last until the next time a Changing Weather result is rolled on the Kickoff Event Table.
