If an opposing player Falls Over as a result of attempting to Dodge, Leap or Jump away from a square in this player's Tackle Zone, this player may use this Skill. If they do, they may apply a +1 modifier to either the Armour Roll or Injury Roll. This modifier may be applied after the roll has been made. If the opposition player suffers a Casualty as a result of a failed Dodge, Leap or Jump away from a square in this player's Tackle Zone, then this player will count as having caused that Casualty and will receive Star Player Points as appropriate.
If a player tries to leave the Tackle Zone of multiple players with this Skill at the same time, only one of those players may use this Skill.
