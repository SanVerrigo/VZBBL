When this player declares a Block Action, they may perform two Block Actions each targeting a different opposition player they are Marking. If they do, then this player will reduce their Strength Characteristic by 2 for the duration of the Block Actions. These Block Actions happen simultaneously, though you may wish to roll them separately for clarity. This means that both Block Actions are resolved in full, even if one of them results in a Turnover. This player cannot Follow-up during either of these Block Actions.
A player with this Skill cannot also have the Frenzy Skill.
